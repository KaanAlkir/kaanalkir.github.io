I am a Mathematics MSc student and teaching assistant at Bilkent University. My research lies at the intersection of inverse reinforcement learning, mean-field games, and reproducing kernel Hilbert space methods. I am broadly interested in reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, game theory, and the mathematical foundations of decision-making under uncertainty. My work aims to combine rigorous mathematical analysis with algorithmic approaches for understanding and designing intelligent behaviour in complex, large-scale systems.
